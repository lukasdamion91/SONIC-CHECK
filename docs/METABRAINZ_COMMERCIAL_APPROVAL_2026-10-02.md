# MetaBrainz commercial approval — effective 2 October 2026

**SONIC CHECK is an active Commercial Bronze supporter of MetaBrainz.**
The completed support agreement and commercial approval are confirmed.
See [MetaBrainz's official Bronze supporters page](https://metabrainz.org/supporters).

The account profile confirms permission to use MetaBrainz-published data,
including dumps released under Creative Commons non-commercial licences.
Nicolás Tamargo of MetaBrainz confirmed account activation and Live Data Feed
token-generation access on 2 October 2026. The former LIMITED status and
exclusion of live replication are superseded.

SONIC CHECK uses MusicBrainz identifiers and metadata for catalogue organisation
and links to AcousticBrainz features. The current API integration remains
metadata enrichment; feed provisioning, replication and additional dataset
activation require separate implementation and verification. This approval
does not establish rights in underlying audio or lyrics or open paid checkout.

The [canonical private approval record](https://github.com/lukasdamion91/sonic-check-api/blob/main/docs/METABRAINZ_COMMERCIAL_APPROVAL_2026-10-02.json)
records the evidence and scope. The [beta register](BETA_ACCEPTANCE_CURRENT.md)
tracks remaining acceptance. Original correspondence and screenshots stay private.
